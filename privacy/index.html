Privacy policy

This website belongs to Yousef Ismail M. Basal, approved driving instructor in Bahrain (instructor code 1057).

What we collect

When you book a consultation we collect the details you enter: your name, WhatsApp number, and optionally your email address and a message.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about driving lessons and your licence process. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we arrange or give your lessons, and delete them when you ask us to.

Advertising and analytics

This site may use Google Ads conversion tracking and cookies to measure how many people book a consultation after visiting from an advert. This data is anonymous and handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

Learning guide

The learning guide saves your language choice and lesson scores in your own browser only. Nothing from the guide is sent to us.

Your choices

To see, change or delete your details, contact your instructor on WhatsApp or by phone.
