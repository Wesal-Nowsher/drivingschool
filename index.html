Road signs in 30 seconds

Something you must not do, or a limit.

Blue circle

Something you must do.

Red triangle

A warning: danger ahead.

Octagon

Upside-down triangle

Rectangle

Information and directions.

Driving test

Common mistakes and how to avoid them

Rolling through a STOP sign

Stop completely behind the line, count to two, look both ways, then go.

Forgetting mirrors

Mirror, signal, then move. Make the head check obvious so the examiner sees it.

Wrong lane at a roundabout

Left lane for left and U-turn, right lane for right and straight. Signal before you exit.

Touching cones when reversing

Go very slowly, use small steering movements and check both mirrors.

Late or missing indicators

Signal early, before you brake or turn, and cancel it afterwards.

Rushing from nerves

Slow and safe always beats fast. Take a breath before you move off.

Test day

How to feel ready on the day

Your test is at the Driving School Directorate in Isa Town. A little preparation makes a big difference.

  • Sleep well and eat before you go
  • Arrive early with your CPR and learning licence
  • Wear comfortable, flat shoes
  • Do your seat, mirror and seat belt checks before moving
  • Listen to the examiner and ask if you did not understand
  • If you make a small mistake, stay calm and keep driving safely

FAQ

Common questions

What do I need to start?

Your original CPR and a copy, and an ATM or debit card for the government fees at the branch.

How much does it cost in total?

The driving classes are 7 BD per class (154 BD for 22 classes). With the government fees (eye test, learning book, test appointment and licence card) the full cost is 217 BD.

Can I pay per class?

Yes. You can pay each class separately, or all 22 hours at once.

Automatic or manual?

Both. Tell us which licence you need when you book your consultation.

Where is the driving test?

At the Driving School Directorate in Isa Town, once your training hours are complete.

What if I fail the test?

You can book the test again.
